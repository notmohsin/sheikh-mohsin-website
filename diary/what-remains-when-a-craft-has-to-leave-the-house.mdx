I spent the last academic year with a thirteen-person team on Taraksi, the silver filigree work we followed through FLAME’s Discover India Program, from September 2025 to April 2026. We read the history and the technique first. Then we designed field surveys and semi-structured interviews, and sat with artisans who could say, in their own words, how a household craft becomes something that has to live as commerce.

I am still not sure the story is only one of progress. A practice that grew up inside a house has to be priced once it leaves that house. Something remains. Something gets sold. I do not yet know how to tell those apart without flattening the people who still make the thing.

We turned what we heard into academic documentation. That write-up is not public. This note is the part I can hold.
